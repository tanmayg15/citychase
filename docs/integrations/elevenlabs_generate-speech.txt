elevenlabs/generate-speech
  Convert text to speech with an ElevenLabs voice, billed per character, returning the audio as a data URL. Free-tier pricing differs from paid-tier pricing.
  billing: paid tier: base $0.000065 per character (some inputs cost less or more); free tier: base $0.0002 per character (some inputs cost less or more)
